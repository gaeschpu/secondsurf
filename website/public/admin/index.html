Price offers

Customers can offer up to 35 % below the price. Accept, decline or make a counter-offer: they get an email in their language.

    Pieces from sellers

    Sellers submit pieces with their own photos. Check the details, change the texts or the price if needed, then approve (the piece goes into the shop) or reject with a reason. They get an email either way. Commission: 5 % on a seller's first 3 sales, then 7 %.

      Payouts

      Sellers ship their pieces themselves. A payout is due as soon as the buyer confirms the parcel, or 14 days after shipping; a problem reported by the buyer holds it. Due payouts go into one payment file for your e-banking.

      Your account for payouts (only saved on this device)

        Orders

        Online orders paid with Stripe. Print the address label and packing slip (or save them as a PDF), stick the label on the parcel, then mark the order as shipped. Postage and the barcode come from Swiss Post (Post-App, online or at the counter).

          Start an auction

          Customers with an account bid with a limit; the site bids for them in CHF 1 steps. A bid in the last minute adds 5 minutes. While the auction runs (and until the winner has paid), the piece can't be bought normally. The winner pays within 48 hours.

          Add a piece

            The first photo is the one on the shop card. Tap ★ to make another one first. Tap No model under a photo to mark it as Woman or Man: logged-in customers who chose women or men see those photos first.

            The AI filled in the form from your photos. Check everything, especially the size, then add the price.

            More details (optional)

            The auction starts as soon as you publish, and the lot shows on the Auctions page. It never appears in the shop and can't be bought at a fixed price. For a bundle, choose size Mix and list the pieces (with their sizes) in the description.
